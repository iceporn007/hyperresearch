## Classification Rules

- Fact: directly stated by a reliable source or directly observed in data.
- Inference: reasoned conclusion drawn from multiple facts.
- Opinion: recommendation, judgment, forecast, or preference.

## Review Checklist

- Every final-report factual claim appears in this table.
- Every high-impact inference lists its supporting facts.
- Contradictory evidence is recorded rather than hidden.
- Confidence levels reflect source quality and agreement.
